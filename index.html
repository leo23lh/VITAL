06How it works

Sourced

Every person and tie cites the pages that state it: official records first, then news outlets across the spectrum. Ties that rest on a single source are drawn dashed and count for less.

Scored

Pull measures access to people in power. Bond measures how deep one tie runs. 2028 backing estimates who is likely to back whom, from ties, politics and what people have said.

Checked

Scores are reasons to look, not facts or forecasts, and each one shows its reasons. They are tested against what happened later: who went on to donate to whom, and which alliances broke.
